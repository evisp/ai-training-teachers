# Prompt library

Ready-made prompts you can copy, fill in and use. Replace everything in `[square brackets]` with your own details.

## A simple formula

| Part | What to write | Example |
|------|---------------|---------|
| Role | Who the AI should act as | *Act as an experienced biology teacher* |
| Task | What you want | *Create a 10-question quiz* |
| Context | Grade, topic, students | *for 8th grade, on photosynthesis* |
| Format | How the answer should look | *as a table with answers at the end* |
